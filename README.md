1.1 Business Problem

Hospitals still use fragmented systems: appointments on paper or in Excel, lab results that are transmitted manually, separate billing from medical history of the patient.

Problems:
High waiting times
Communication errors between departments
Impossibility of having a broad unified oveview of the patient.

A healthcare provider (national or private) needs a system in which each department (Laboratory, Pharmacy, Financial, Hospital management) can evolve and scale independently.
